# Design: DrukStock Interview Demo Page (3D + flat)

Generated by /office-hours on 2026-10-23
Branch: main
Repo: tshewangyeshi/DrukStock
Status: APPROVED
Mode: Startup

## Problem Statement
The founder asked for "a 3d intelligent webpage" that looks "so professional and not AI generated", as the DrukStock landing page. The session found that the page's real viewer is a shopkeeper looking at the founder's phone during the Phase 0 interviews (founder: "shopkeepers during interviews, on my phone, tap try it"). DrukStock's approved plan builds no app screens during Phase 0, so "Try it" would otherwise have nothing to open. The page therefore becomes a hands-on demo of the credit book: it gives the shopkeeper something credible to touch, and gives the founder evidence of what shopkeepers actually do.

## Demand Evidence
None yet. This page is a tool for collecting it during the Phase 0 interviews defined in `docs/designs/drukstock-core-design.md` (The Assignment).

## Status Quo
Shopkeepers keep credit in a paper baki ledger (founder belief, unverified). Without this page, the founder can only describe DrukStock in words during interviews.

## Target User & Narrowest Wedge
- **Viewer:** a Thimphu grocery or hardware shopkeeper during an interview, using the founder's phone.
- **One action:** tap "Try it", record a credit in the demo, and see the total and the overdue flag change.
- **Narrowest version:** a single page that works offline, holds fake data only, and needs no backend.

## Constraints
- It runs on the founder's phone, often inside a shop with weak or no signal. After the first load it must work fully offline (service worker).
- First view within about 3 s on mobile data when online. Size budgets and the WebGL fallback are defined in the Demo Spec.
- No shopkeeper data is collected or stored. The only stored data is the founder's own interaction log, kept in that browser only.
- "Professional, not AI-generated": real local detail (Nu amounts, Bhutanese names, a real shop counter), one restrained palette, deliberate typography, and none of the generic glowing-orb, purple-gradient or floating-blob look. Dzongkha labels must be written or checked by a native speaker.
- It is separate from the DrukStock app and does not touch the Phase 0 foundation (schema, RLS, auth, sync).

## Premises
1. The viewer is a shopkeeper during an interview on the founder's phone, with one action: "Try it".
2. Speed beats spectacle: about 3 s first view, about 500 KB of 3D, and a static fallback.
3. **Revised after the second opinion:** "Try it" opens a hands-on credit demo with fake data and no backend, in **two variants sharing one demo engine**: a light 3D shop-counter scene and a flat ruled-notebook version. Assignment, metrics and the decision rule are in the Demo Spec.
4. "Professional, not AI-generated" means specific and restrained (see Constraints).
5. It is a separate small site on the same Cloudflare Pages host and doesn't touch Phase 0.

## Cross-Model Perspective
An independent Claude subagent (cold read):
- **Steelman:** "This isn't really a landing page. It's an interview prop: a credit book the shopkeeper can touch... so that within 30 seconds they're recording 'Karma Dorji, Nu 450' and reacting to it."
- **Key insight:** because the founder holds the phone, there are no visitors to win over. "The only thing that counts is the 'Try it' demo", so the page should be built to measure what shopkeepers do.
- **Challenged premise 3:** a 3D book may add steps before the one action that matters and test visual impressiveness rather than switching. Proposed test: alternate 3D and flat across interviews and compare time to first credit, help needed, unprompted real-use questions, and follow-up interest. The founder accepted (D19).
- **Bugs it caught:** "WhatsApp us" would open on the founder's own phone (needs a QR code); the shop may have no signal (needs offline).
- **Its 48h build:** plain HTML/CSS/JS, CSS 3D only, a service worker, under 150 KB, plus Skip 30 days, Reset, `?v=flat`, a founder-only log and a WhatsApp QR code.

## Approaches Considered
- **A: One-scene demo page.** Not chosen as the primary; kept as the plain 2D fallback idea, which became the flat variant.
- **B: Astro scroll story + demo.** Not chosen: too much page for a viewer who only needs the demo.
- **C: The demo IS the page.** CHOSEN (founder: "go with C").

## Recommended Approach
**Approach C, with both variants (D19).**

**Flow (approved wireframe, 4 states):**
1. **Opening:** one light 3D shop-counter scene with a closed red baki notebook and slow parallax that follows a finger drag, with a gentle idle drift (eng review D28: no motion permission, so no popup ever), plus one line ("Your baki book, on your phone.") and a "Try it" button. If WebGL fails, a still image of the same scene shows and the tap still works.
2. **Demo:** the notebook opens into 6 made-up customers with Bhutanese names and Nu balances. A large keypad and "Gave credit" / "Got payment" update the customer's balance and the shop total with a short animation. "One month later →" moves the demo clock so the overdue flag appears (design review D12); Reset lives only on the founder screens, and "Next shopkeeper" resets automatically (D11).
3. **Flat variant (`?v=flat`):** the same engine and data drawn as a ruled paper notebook with clear printed tabular figures and one ink color (design review D16). No WebGL.
4. **End card:** "Want this for your shop?" with a QR code that opens WhatsApp on the shopkeeper's own phone (eng review D25: a separate DrukStock number, accepted as public; QR encodes `wa.me/<number>?text=DrukStock%20demo`; the number is also printed in large digits under the QR for shopkeepers without WhatsApp), and "Try again". A long-press on the logo opens the **founder log** (fields and durability rules in the Demo Spec). It can be exported as CSV or cleared, and stays in that browser only.

**Build shape:**
- One static site in `site/` (separate from the app), deployed to Cloudflare Pages.
- A shared demo engine (pure JS: fake ledger state, balance math, a 30-day skip, logging), with two renderers: 3D and flat.
- **Engine rules (eng review D27, mirroring the app):** Nu amounts use a decimal library; overpayment is allowed and shown as "Advance Nu X", applied FIFO; "Correct" creates a reversal shown struck through, each entry reversible once. An empty or 0 amount disables both buttons; amounts above Nu 9,99,999 are rejected. The demo ledger lives in memory only (reload or Reset restores the fixture); only the founder log persists. All 6 starting customers live in `site/fixtures/customers.json`. **Visibility (eng review D37):** the main screen shows only the customer list, keypad, "Gave credit" and "Got payment"; "Correct" appears only after an entry in a customer's history is tapped, and "Advance Nu X" appears only as a balance label when it happens.
- **Before building the 3D renderer (eng review D23):** a 2-hour throwaway spike measures the Three.js scene's gzipped size and frame rate on the founder's phone; the renderer choice is reopened with those numbers.
- 3D renderer: a single small Three.js scene (counter, shelf and notebook built in code from simple shapes and textured with real photos the founder takes in a Thimphu shop, with permission; no model file, per eng review D33), loaded only for the 3D variant and only for the opening and transition (see Demo Spec). Budgets are defined in the Demo Spec.
- **Service worker (eng review D24):** the install step precaches every asset of both variants; the founder start screen shows "Offline ready · build <hash>" only once that finishes. The cache name includes the build hash; a new worker precaches, then calls `skipWaiting()` and `clients.claim()` and deletes old caches. Required test: after one online visit, in airplane mode, both `?v=3d` and `?v=flat` load fully.

## Demo Spec (definitions both renderers and the tests share)

**What is 3D vs shared (R1-8).** WebGL draws only the opening counter scene and the notebook-opening transition. The customer list, keypad, buttons and end card are HTML/CSS shared by both variants through the demo engine. In the 3D variant that shared UI sits inside a notebook frame with a slight CSS 3D tilt; in the flat variant it sits on a ruled-paper background. So the comparison is "3D opening + 3D frame" against "flat notebook", with the same interaction underneath.

**Fallback (R1-9; timing per eng review D29).** If WebGL is unavailable, or the first 3D frame hasn't rendered within 1.5 s of the "Hand over" tap (enabled only after "Offline ready"), the 3D variant shows a still image of the counter scene (it then stays for the whole interview) and continues with the same HTML demo. The log records `renderer=fallback` for that session, and fallback sessions are excluded from the 3D-vs-flat comparison.

**Variant assignment (R1-6).** The page alternates automatically by interview number (see Sessions and metrics): odd interviews are 3D and even interviews are flat. The founder log shows "Next: 3D / flat", with a one-tap override that works offline. `?v=3d` / `?v=flat` still force a variant. Each shopkeeper sees exactly one variant; if the founder shows the other one afterwards, that second session is marked `repeat` and excluded.

**Sessions and metrics (R1-3).**
- An interview starts when the founder taps "Hand over" and ends when the founder taps "Next shopkeeper". Reset and "Try again" inside an interview restart the demo but not the interview.
- **Time to first credit (eng review D34):** seconds from the shopkeeper's **first touch** anywhere on the opening screen after the founder's "Hand over" tap (the same event in both variants) to the first committed "Gave credit" with an amount above zero. The time from "Hand over" to that first touch is logged separately and never used by the decision rule.
- **Interviews, not sessions, drive alternation:** the variant advances only when the founder taps **"Next shopkeeper"** in the log. Reload, Reset and "Try again" keep the same interview number and variant.
- **Tap count:** taps between session start and that first credit.
- **Abandoned:** the session ends without any committed credit.

**Founder-entered fields (R1-2, R1-4).** After the shopkeeper hands the phone back, the founder opens the log with a **two-finger** 1.5 s long-press, available only on the end card or the founder start screen, never on demo screens (eng review D38). The log opens on the current session with three one-tap fields: **needed help** (yes/no), **asked a real-use question unprompted** (yes/no plus an optional note, for example "asked about reminders"), **wants follow-up** (yes/no), and **repeat** (yes/no; set automatically to yes when the variant override or a `?v=` force is used after this shopkeeper already had a counted interview). The shopkeeper never sees these.

**Decision rule (R1-1), fixed before the interviews.** Only non-fallback, non-repeat sessions count, and each variant needs at least 4.
- **Primary metric:** share of sessions with a first credit recorded without help.
- **Secondary metric:** median time to first credit.
- **3D carries forward only if** its no-help share is at least flat's and its median time is no more than 2 s slower.
- **Otherwise flat carries forward.** That includes mixed or tied results, since flat is cheaper to build and maintain.
- **If either variant has fewer than 4 counted sessions after the 10 interviews,** run extra interviews with the same alternation until both reach 4, capped at 4 extra interviews; if still short, flat carries forward.
- Real-use questions and follow-up interest are reported alongside, not used to decide.

**Log durability (R1-5).** Install the page to the phone's home screen, and call `navigator.storage.persist()` the first time the founder opens the log. The log header shows the stored session count and the last export date. Export CSV (via the share sheet) after each interview day. Losing the log is treated as losing Phase 0 evidence. **Single home (eng review D35):** "Hand over" and the log work only in the installed home-screen app; in a normal browser tab the start screen says "Open DrukStock from your home screen" and won't start an interview. The `persist()` result is checked; if refused, the start screen warns "Log may be cleared: export after every interview".

**Overdue rule and starting data (R1-7).**
- Six customers with Bhutanese names. Two start overdue: Karma Dorji, with unpaid credit dated 41 days ago, and Tashi Lhamo, at 35 days. The other four are under 30 days.
- A customer is overdue when any credit is still unpaid more than 30 days after its date. Payments clear the oldest credits first, so a large enough payment can clear the flag.
- "Skip 30 days" moves the demo clock forward 30 days on each tap. Reset restores the starting data and today's date.

**Size budgets (R1-10).** Budgets are gzipped transfer size of everything each variant loads, including fonts, images, scripts, the model and the fallback image:
- **3D variant: ≤ 500 KB** [estimated split: Three.js core ~170 KB gz, model ≤ 150 KB meshopt-compressed, rest ~180 KB];
- **flat variant: ≤ 150 KB**.
- A CI check measures both on every PR and fails when either is over budget.

**Labels (R1-11).** The headline, "Try it", "Gave credit", "Got payment" and the end-card line are bilingual, Dzongkha above English, with no language toggle. All other text is English. A native speaker writes and checks every Dzongkha string before the first interview. **Dzongkha rendering (amended by eng review D36):** a Tibetan-script webfont subset at build time that keeps every OpenType layout feature and every glyph those features can reach (not only the typed codepoints); a Playwright CI test screenshots each bilingual string and compares it with reference images the native speaker approved once. Originally: a subset to the glyphs in the fixed bilingual strings (estimated ≤ 20 KB gzipped), counted in both variant budgets and checked on the founder's phone before the first interview.

**Eng re-review amendments.**
- **Navigation (ER-D1):** one History API entry per screen; `popstate` moves within the app (customer page → list, end card → list). On the opening and list screens during an interview, back re-pushes and stays put; only the founder start screen lets back leave the app.
- **Budget allocation (ER-D2):** both Plex weights are subset at build time to the Latin characters used (A-Z, a-z, tabular digits, Nu punctuation), alongside the D36 Jomolhari subset. CI checks gzipped lines: fonts ≤60 KB total, app JS+CSS ≤50 KB, fixture/QR/icons ≤20 KB, flat headroom ≥20 KB. The 3D variant adds Three.js and textures within its 500 KB. Plex sizes are [re-measure before gate].
- **Decimal library (ER-D3):** big.js (≈2.7 KB gzipped per its README) for all Nu arithmetic, also the planned library for the app client.
- **Engine clock:** injectable demo clock (required by design D12 "One month later").

**Problem**

### Scope Challenge A: assessment

**What already solves each sub-problem (search check):**
- Offline precaching and updates: Workbox precaching treats the manifest as the source of truth for each version, uses hashed URLs, and revisions HTML by content hash ([Workbox precaching](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/workbox/modules/workbox-precaching.md.txt), [web.dev](https://web.dev/precache-with-workbox)).
